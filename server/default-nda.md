This Non-Disclosure Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into between CLASSENCE, an educational technology venture founded by Yusuf Abdulwahab Boluwatife, operating in Lagos State, Nigeria, and the Core Team member who electronically accepts and signs this Agreement (the “Recipient”).

1. PURPOSE

The purpose of this Agreement is to protect Classence's confidential business, technical, financial, operational, and user-related information accessed by Core Team members during their involvement with the organization.

2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

Confidential Information includes, but is not limited to:

- Source code, repositories, databases, APIs, algorithms, product designs, and unreleased features.
- Business strategies, financial records, pricing, partnership discussions, and future plans.
- Student information, educational content, user data, and internal analytics.
- Internal meetings, documents, communications, credentials, and operational procedures.
- Confidential information belonging to Classence's partners and collaborators.

3. RECIPIENT'S OBLIGATIONS

The Recipient agrees to:

- Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential.
- Use confidential materials only for authorized Classence activities.
- Not disclose, copy, distribute, publish, or exploit confidential information without written authorization.
- Not share internal meeting recordings, documents, credentials, or private repositories with unauthorized persons.
- Protect Classence's intellectual property and report suspected security breaches promptly.
- Not misuse confidential information for personal gain or unfair competition.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All intellectual property owned by Classence remains its property. Access to confidential materials does not grant ownership or commercial rights.

Ownership of new contributions shall be determined by separate written agreements and applicable law.

5. DATA PROTECTION

The Recipient shall protect student information, user records, and other personal data in accordance with applicable Nigerian data protection laws, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

6. EXCEPTIONS

Confidentiality obligations do not apply to information lawfully available to the public, independently developed without using Classence's confidential information, lawfully obtained without restrictions, or approved for disclosure in writing.

Legally required or protected disclosures are permitted.

7. TERMINATION AND DURATION

Upon leaving Classence or losing authorization, the Recipient must return or securely delete confidential materials under their control, subject to legal retention requirements.

Confidentiality obligations remain effective throughout membership and for five (5) years after departure. Legally protected trade secrets remain confidential for as long as the law permits.

8. BREACH OF AGREEMENT

Any unauthorized disclosure or misuse may result in suspension, termination of access or membership, and appropriate legal action under Nigerian law.

9. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Disputes shall first be addressed amicably before pursuing lawful remedies.

10. ACCEPTANCE

By electronically signing this Agreement, the Recipient confirms that they have read, understood, and voluntarily agreed to its terms.

Electronic signatures and associated signing records may serve as evidence of acceptance, subject to applicable law.
